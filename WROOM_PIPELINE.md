# Wroom — Document Pipeline Spec
*Zen Story Architect × Warner Bros. Greenlight Edition*

---

## Document Pipeline (in order)

1. Premise Document
2. Tri-Axial Audit
3. Character Dossiers
4. One-Pager
5. 37-Beat Outline (Greenlight Beat Sheet)
6. Treatment (5–10 pages)
7. Pitch Package (One-Pager + Treatment + Sizzle Script + Deck)
8. Fountain Script

All documents are partner-facing. Every document is a discrete, complete deliverable.

---

## Document Specs

### 1. Premise Document

- **Logline** — One sentence. Hero + want + obstacle + stakes.
- **Genre** — Primary genre, secondary if hybrid (e.g., Sci-Fi Thriller, Dramedy)
- **Tone** — Emotional register and visual feel (e.g., grounded and paranoid, warm and mythic)
- **World** — Where and when. The rules of the reality.
- **Comps** — 2–3 titles. Format: "[Title] meets [Title]" or "[Title] in the world of [Title]"

---

### 2. Tri-Axial Audit
*Clean report. Partner-ready. No exposed reasoning — authoritative conclusions only.*

- **Plot Type** — The selected lens from the 7 Plots, one sentence on why it fits
- **Zen Engine Arc** — The specific Ego → Connection journey required for this story
- **Macro-Dopamine Forecast** — Three bullets: where Novelty, Meaning, and Suspense each live across the three acts

---

### 3. Character Dossiers
*One per major character. Partner-facing.*

#### Standard Format (Protagonist / Supporting / Sub-Protagonist)

**[Name] — [Archetype Tagline]**

**The Vibe**
1–2 paragraph narrative portrait:
- *Visual Fingerprint* — Appearance as a reflection of their Insecurity
- *Environmental Relationship* — How they treat the world (dominate, hide, reorganize?)
- *The Flaw* — The specific named flavor of their Insecurity

**PRIEPIOTS Table**

| Component | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| P — Problem | The specific external challenge |
| R — Reason | The Role/Archetype & Relatability |
| I — Insecurity | The inner inadequacy or psychological wound |
| E — Emotion | Behavioral response when Insecurity is triggered |
| P — Pivot | The ego death moment |
| I — Intention | Specific goal to solve the Problem |
| O — Obstacle | External force blocking the Intention |
| T — Tactic | How they try to overcome the Obstacle |
| S — Sacrifice | The cost of the transformation |

#### Antagonist Additions (after PRIEPIOTS table)

**The Hero of Their Own Story**
1–2 sentences. Their internal justification. Why, from their perspective, they are right.

**The Exploit**
1 sentence. The specific Hero Insecurity they target and the Tactic they use to attack it.

---

### 4. One-Pager
*Partner-facing. Based on Industrial Scripts 14-element template.*

1. **Audience** — Who this is pitched to (1 sentence, personalized per recipient)
2. **Genre** — Primary + secondary genre
3. **Antecedents** — 2–3 comp titles that set tone and marketplace position
4. **The "What If"** — The central premise question that makes the story irresistible
5. **Logline** — One sentence: Hero + want + obstacle + stakes
6. **Protagonist** — Name, archetype, one-line essence
7. **Call to Adventure** — The inciting incident
8. **Desire** — What the hero wants (external goal)
9. **Conflict** — The force opposing them
10. **Action** — How they fight back
11. **The Major Setback** — The all-is-lost moment
12. **The Climax** — The final confrontation
13. **Deeper Meaning** — The theme / what the audience takes home
14. **The Awkward Segue** — Brief personal note: why you, why now, invitation to read

---

### 5. 37-Beat Outline — The Greenlight Beat Sheet
*One approved beat at a time. South Park Logic Gate applied to every beat before approval.*

**Output format per beat:**

```
[SEQUENCE X : BEAT NAME]

THE CONNECTOR: [THEREFORE / BUT — blank for Beat 1]

LOCATION: [INT./EXT. LOCATION - DAY/NIGHT]

CHARACTERS: [All active characters]

THE ACTION: [Precise visual description. Cinematic verbs. External action.]

THE OPPOSITION: [Counter-Move / Modifier Pressure / Environmental Block]

THE ZEN PIVOT:
[Hero: toward Connection or regression to Ego]
[Antagonist: Calcification of Ego]
[Sub-Protagonist: toward Connection or regression to Ego]

STAKES UPDATE: [New information revealed + cost of failure]

ENTERTAINMENT FACTOR: [Novelty / Meaning / Suspense]
```

---

### 6. Treatment (5–10 pages)
*Straight prose narrative. Present tense, active voice, cinematic register.*

- **Act One** — header break
- **Act Two** — header break
- **Act Three** — header break

No structural markup. No beat labels. Reads like a story, not a blueprint.

### 7. Pitch Package
*Four discrete deliverables bundled together:*

#### 7a. One-Pager
*(See spec above)*

#### 7b. Treatment
*(See spec above)*

#### 7c. Sizzle Script
*Written teaser/trailer script. Production-ready for Seedance or VEO.*
- Scene-by-scene visual direction
- VO lines or title cards
- Music/tone notes
- Target runtime: 60–90 seconds when produced

#### 7d. Deck
*Visual presentation. Slides in order:*
1. **Title** — Hero slide, title treatment
2. **Logline** — One sentence, full page
3. **Key Visuals** — Tone/mood board, visual references
4. **Characters** — One slide per major character: name, archetype, one-line essence
5. **World** — Setting, rules of reality, visual palette
6. **Comps** — 2–3 antecedents with brief positioning note
7. **Team** — Writers, producers, key collaborators

### 8. Fountain Script
*Standard Fountain format. No additional metadata or markup.*

## Workflow Rules (Zen Story Architect Protocol)

- **Single Question Rule** — One question at a time. No stacking.
- **South Park Logic Gate** — Every beat (except Beat 1) must connect via THEREFORE or BUT. AND THEN is forbidden.
- **Two-Scene Threshold** — Any supporting character in more than 2 scenes = Sub-Protagonist. Full PRIEPIOTS required.
- **Dopamine Constraint** — Every scene must deliver Novelty, Meaning, or Suspense. Scenes lacking all three = False Dopamine (Mu). Reject and revise.
- **Pincer Protocol** — Modifier + Antagonist must both be active, squeezing the hero.
- **Shadow Protocol** — Antagonist scenes without the Hero: Antagonist faces opposition, rejects the lesson → Calcification of Ego.
- **Supporter Protocol** — Sub-Protagonist scenes without Hero or Antagonist: arc progresses toward Connection, but never exceeds the Hero's dopamine yield.
- **First Appearance Protocol** — Major character's first scene triggers a Character Portrait: Visual Fingerprint + Environmental Relationship + The Tell.
